# Butler Screen Capture & Recording Skill

此技能提供跨平台屏幕截图与画面录制功能，包含：
- 全屏截图 (Full Screenshot)
- 区域截屏 (Area Screenshot with Live Dimension Display)
- 长截屏 (Long Scrolling Screenshot)
- 屏幕录制 (Full & Area Screen Recording)
